    What file does it expect?

    Any export with one row per sleep segment works. In the iOS Shortcuts app: Find Health Samples (type: Sleep) → save Start Date, End Date and Value as CSV or JSON.

    Columns are detected automatically. Recognised stages: Core, Deep, REM, Asleep, In Bed, Awake. Segments from the same night are merged into one summary, and naps are ignored.

    Also accepted: Health Auto Export sleep summaries and backups exported from this app. Re-importing a night replaces it but keeps your tags.

    One-tap sync from iPhone: a Shortcut can open this page with the data attached as #import= followed by the same CSV, Base64-encoded. The nights are imported automatically and removed from the address bar. The data after # never leaves your phone.
